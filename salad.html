Cesar Salad

Cesar Salad is a classic and delicious salad that's perfect for any occasion. This recipe will guide you through making a creamy and flavorful ceasar salad from scratch.

Ingredients:

  • 1 head romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1/2 cup croutons
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions:

  1. Wash and dry the romaine lettuce.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, lemon juice, garlic, mustard, salt, and pepper.
  3. Add the chopped lettuce and croutons to the dressing and toss to coat.
  4. Sprinkle the parmesan cheese over the top and serve immediately.
